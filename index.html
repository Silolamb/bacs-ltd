Our Story

Who We Are

Nassau has no shortage of equipment sellers. What it has always lacked is proper dealer support — the kind where someone actually picks up the phone, knows the product, stocks the parts, and stands behind what they sold you.

That gap is why BACS Ltd. exists.

"The right equipment, properly supported, by people who know The Bahamas."

We are Nassau's only authorized ECHO dealer and an authorized Toro dealer — meaning every product carries the full US manufacturer warranty honored right here on Carmichael Road. No shipping. No grey market. No runaround. Just real support from people who live and work in the same community you do.

Our team services and repairs what we sell. We stock genuine OEM parts. We answer the phone. We respond on WhatsApp. We show up. In a market where after-sale support is the weakest link — that is our strongest differentiator.

From homeowners in Nassau to commercial operators across the Family Islands, we carry the brands that work in Bahamian conditions — built for salt air, humidity, and the demands of island life. Come see us on Carmichael Road, across from Lickety Split.
