Our Story

Who We Are

Nassau has no shortage of equipment sellers. What it has always lacked is a proper dealership — one that stocks the right brands, knows the products, carries the parts, and actually stands behind what it sold you long after the sale.

That is exactly what BACS Ltd. was built to be.

"Multiple brands. One team. Fully supported across The Bahamas."

We represent multiple world-class brands spanning outdoor power, turf and grounds, golf carts, agricultural equipment, commercial vehicles, trailers, flood control, and clean energy — bringing genuine parts, factory warranty support, and real after-sale service to Nassau and the Family Islands.

Every product we sell carries the full US manufacturer warranty — honored locally. No shipping. No grey market.

We service what we sell. We stock genuine OEM parts. We deliver to the Family Islands and keep equipment running after it gets there. When you need us, we answer — on the phone, on WhatsApp, in person. That is not a promise we make lightly. In this market, it is what sets us apart.

From homeowners in Nassau to commercial operators, government agencies, and businesses across the Family Islands — we carry the brands built for Bahamian conditions. Get in touch and let us show you what proper dealer support looks like.
