How It Works

Natural Science.
Proven Results.

Ecomo uses no electronic components — no capacitors, no IC chips, nothing to fail. Instead it harnesses the natural electrical properties of two powerful minerals to lubricate the flow of electricity through your existing transformer.

💎
Tourmaline

Known in Japan as the "electric stone" — generates a weak 0.06mA electric field and emits electrons when stimulated. This lubricates the flow of electricity through wires and equipment.

🧲
Ferrite

A highly effective noise-reducing magnetic material composed of iron oxide, cobalt, nickel, and manganese. Removes electrical noise and interference for cleaner, more efficient power flow.

⚡
Combined Effect

Together, tourmaline and ferrite facilitate efficient and stable transformer operation — leading to 5–15% lower electricity consumption with no changes to your existing infrastructure.

Installation

Installed in 30–60 Minutes.
No Downtime Required.

Ecomo connects directly to the secondary side of your existing transformer. No rewiring, no external power source, no UPS certification required. The acrylic-coated interior and cast nylon housing provide high insulation and durability in Bahamian conditions.
